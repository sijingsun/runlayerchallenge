1
Friction observed
With "Requests" nav removed, there is no persistent signal that a request is waiting. Admin may miss it entirely.
Potential idea
Persistent notification badge on the renamed admin entry point (e.g., "Admin" not "Settings") showing pending request count — visible from anywhere in the product.
2
Friction observed
Queue is a flat list — every request looks identical. No basis for triage.
3 signals to add to each card
① Priority flag — Jane marks "High priority" at submit. Shows as ! badge top-right of card.

② Wait time — elapsed time since submission. "3 days" grows naturally.

③ Risk level — High (risky tools + no org precedent) or Low (existing approvals + few risky tools). Combo of both factors.
6
Friction observed
Same connector requested repeatedly by different people. Admin reviews the same decision over and over instead of making a group-level policy once.
Potential idea
Pattern-triggered prompt: "8 people requested Linear #2 this month — consider granting it to the Engineering group." Also: Step 2 of the grant modal surfaces similar waiting users to batch-approve in one action.

9
Friction observed
Flow 1 Overview shows no security summary — risk is only a "3" badge on a tab, easy to miss. Flow 2 shows a security summary inline. Same admin, same decision weight, different information.
Potential idea
Elevate security summary into the Overview for both flows equally. One clear signal, same position, same format — linked directly to risk detail without requiring tab navigation.
→
Click "Grant access"
more steps follow
5
Friction observed
"Grant access" reads as a terminal action. Admin clicks it expecting to be done — a configuration modal appears unexpectedly. The two steps are correct; the button label isn't.
Potential idea
"Approve & configure →" or "Continue to access setup →" — both signal more is coming. Step 2 modal opens with "Configure access for Jane Cooper" to orient the admin.

Step 2 is also a good place to suggest: "Priya and Marcus are also waiting for Linear #2 — add them here?"
→
36-tool checklist
permission config
7
Friction observed
Every approval forces the admin to face a 36-tool checklist regardless of intent. Most admins want one-click approve with the connector version's default profile.
Potential idea
One-click approve with the default toolset as the primary action. "Customize access" is a secondary expansion — available but not forced. Fast approvals stay fast.
11
Friction observed
The permission config modal doesn't show which AI clients this approval will apply to. The admin can't confirm or adjust client scope at the point of approval.
Potential idea
Surface Jane's selected AI clients prominently in the approval modal — "Requested for: ChatGPT · Cursor" — so the admin can approve as-is or narrow the scope before confirming.
